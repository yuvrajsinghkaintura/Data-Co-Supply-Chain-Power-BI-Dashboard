## 📊 1. Core Architectural Pillars & Metrics

This analytics framework moves away from generic isolated reports to follow a continuous operational flow layout across five customized data perspectives. The layout enforces a strict structural standard using 10px rounded container boundaries and customized macro-navigation flow arrows to maintain storytelling alignment.

## 💡 2. Executive Business Insights (Recruiter-Ready)

* **Route Bottlenecks Identified:** The *Regional Delivery Risk Matrix* isolated an operational delay patterns in Central Africa and the Caribbean, flagging corridors that breached our critical **54.80% risk threshold**.

* **Profit Margin Protection:** While the global average profit margin holds stable at **12.00%**, scatter plot mapping successfully separated high-performing product lines from raw material procurement bleeders.

* **Inventory Distribution Alignment:** Cross-analyzing a **384K inventory footprint** against **180.52K total active orders** revealed localized stock placement deficits, pointing directly to a need for decentralized supplier routing.
